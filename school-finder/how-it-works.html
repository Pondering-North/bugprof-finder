How GradHunt 20/20 Works

Our Purpose

GradHunt 20/20 makes it easy for prospective graduate students to explore their options and plan their future education. Type any question about a school, a field, or a specific program, and you'll get a ranked list of programs best suited for your goals.

The results are generated by Claude AI from a comprehensive database of US and Canadian research universities. All results focus on the schools most likely to offer well-funded graduate research positions.

How to Search

  1. Type a question or keyword into the search box on the Search Programs page.
  2. GradHunt sends your query to Claude AI, which returns up to 10 ranked programs with descriptions and links.
  3. Browse the results to find which card best fits your needs
  4. Click "+ Add to Ento-Professo" on any card to send that school to the Ento-Professo faculty browser.
  5. Visit My Roadmap if you want to create a personalized career plan before you start your search.

Search Tips

Be specific. "Best PhD programs in chemical ecology" will give you much better results than "best biology schools".

Try different angles. You can search by field, career goal, geography, or degree type; for example, "master's programs in public health in Canada" or "schools where I can study vector-borne disease".

Use the Quick Search buttons in the sidebar as starting points. You can refine from there.

Ento-Professo integration. Once you've added schools, switch over to Ento-Professo to browse actual faculty profiles.
