Quick Searches
Find Your Graduate Program

Ask anything — a field, a specialty, a career goal, or a country preference. For example: "best PhD programs in chemical ecology", "top schools for wildlife conservation in Canada", or "where should I go for a master's in public health?"

OUR PURPOSE:

GradSearch 20/20 uses artificial intelligence to match your interests to graduate programs across the United States and Canada. Type any question — a field, a specialty, a career goal — and get a ranked list of relevant programs.

OUR METHOD:

Results are generated by Claude AI from its knowledge of US and Canadian research universities. Click Add to Ento-Professo on any card to send that school to the main faculty search tool, where you can scrape and browse actual faculty profiles.

Search Tips

Be specific:
"best PhD programs in chemical ecology" beats "best biology schools"

Try Canada:
Add "in Canada" to find programs at Canadian universities

Career goals work:
"schools for someone who wants to work in wildlife conservation"

Add to Ento-Professo:
Found a school? Click the green button to send it to the faculty browser
